Yes, even in January

Michigan topsoil only freezes a few inches down, so winter is mostly a matter of the right tools.

  • Pilot holes. We drill a quiet pilot hole through the frozen crust and press the stakes in by hand.
  • Two stakes per letter. From November to March, so lake wind can't knock them over. On ice or a driveway we use weighted stands.
  • Free snow re-set. If fresh snow or a plow buries it overnight, we come back in the morning and fix it.

      Questions and house rules

      How setup works, what we won't spell, and what happens if the house isn't thrilled.

      Before you book

      Does someone at the house have to know?

      Yes. If you don't live there, you name one adult who does, and we text them before setup. It keeps surprises welcome and gives us someone to call. The only exception is when you live at the address yourself.

      What messages won't you put up?

      We can turn down any message. We never spell out news someone hasn't shared yet, like a pregnancy or a health update, and nothing meant to embarrass or insult.

      We also turn down romantic messages for someone you're not in a relationship with, orders where you can't name anyone who lives there, and repeat orders aimed at the same house.

      We rent. Can we still book?

      Yes, with the landlord's or HOA's okay. The booking form asks you to confirm it for rentals, apartments and condos.

      How far ahead do I need to book?

      By 6 PM the day before is ideal. Under 48 hours' notice adds a $20 rush fee, as long as we have the letters free that night. The booking form checks as you type.

      On the night

      Will you wake anyone up?

      We try hard not to. We park a house down with the engine and headlights off, work by red headlamp, and press stakes in by hand. Most setups take 10 to 15 minutes between 11 PM and 3 AM.

      Where on the lawn does it go?

      On the lawn itself, at least 5 feet back from the sidewalk. Never on the grass strip between the sidewalk and the curb, since that belongs to the city. We keep stakes away from gas meters and utility boxes.

      Can the stakes damage anything?

      Our stakes go 10 to 12 inches down. Tell us about sprinkler lines, landscape lighting wire or an invisible dog fence when you book, and we'll work around them.

      Why isn't your logo on the display?

      The display is about them, not us. Our name only appears on the small card at the door.

      After the reveal

      What if they don't like it?

      Text the number on the card and we'll remove it within two hours, no questions asked. They can also pull the stakes and lean everything by the porch, and we'll collect it. We won't argue about it, and we won't tell them who ordered it.

      Do I get a refund if it comes down early?

      If it comes down because no one at the house actually agreed, there's no refund. For anything else, like a shy birthday kid, we'll offer a partial credit. After a recent loss or hard news at the house, we refund in full.

      Do I get a photo?

      Yes. We photograph every finished display and email it to you with the takedown time.

      Weather, winter and payment

      Do you set up in winter?

      All year. In frozen ground we drill a pilot hole and press the stakes in. From November through March every letter gets two stakes against the wind. On ice or a driveway, we use weighted stands.

      What if it snows overnight?

      If fresh snow or a plow buries the display, we come back the next morning and fix it for free.

      How do I pay?

      By card through Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our servers. Stripe emails your receipt after takedown.
